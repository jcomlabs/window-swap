## Intent

Create a factual Build Week demonstration, not a glossy advertisement. Start with
the friction of stacked snapped windows, show the released Windows application
working, then prove the meaningful work completed during the submission period:
negative-coordinate monitor support, duplicate-instance protection, deterministic
tests, privacy-conscious diagnostics, CI, and a downloadable verified release.

## Assets

- Sanitized local screen recording — the real `C:\WWS\window_swapper.exe`
  operating only on disposable demonstration windows.
- Public repository metadata from `https://github.com/jcomlabs/window-swap`.

## Customizations

- Use the product's blue `W` tray-icon language as the visual anchor.
- Include readable English captions because judges may watch without sound.
- Show the before/after distinction between the June prototype and the July 18
  Build Week extension without exposing the unsafe private legacy history.

## Notes

- No personal desktop, window titles, contacts, photos, credentials, private paths,
  or notifications may appear in the video.
- No copyrighted music or third-party brand marks.
- Keep the final video below three minutes; target roughly 100 seconds.
- The narration line that names GPT-5.6 must be owner-verified before public upload.
